Every reader app speaks 90 Fed. Reg. 898 as "ninety fed reg eight ninety eight" and reads URLs character by character. This page runs the same normalize.py that the milisten CLI uses — the real file, executed in your browser through Pyodide, not a re-implementation that can drift.

Nothing leaves your machine. There is no server: your text, your PDF, and the speech all stay in this tab.

What this page cannot do

      
    
        
  • Fetch a URL. Browsers block cross-origin reads, so there is no
          way to pull an article without a server. Download it and drop the file in.
    • 
        
  • Produce a chaptered .m4b. That needs ffmpeg. The
          CLI does it; a browser tab cannot.
    • 
        
  • Use the good voice. This plays through your operating system's
          built-in voices. The CLI renders with Kokoro, which sounds markedly better.
    •
